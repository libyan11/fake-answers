# Fake Answers

A bluffing party game for phones, part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

Every question is a strange but true fact with a word missing. Everyone writes a fake answer that sounds real, then all the answers are mixed together and everyone tries to spot the truth.

- Spot the truth: 2 points. Every player who picks your fake: 1 point for you.
- Write the real answer by accident and you're asked to write another.
- Matching fakes merge into one, and both writers score.
- With few players the game adds its own fakes, so there are always at least 4 answers.
- English or Arabic questions, 5 to 15 per game, a write timer, and an optional double-points last question.
- Online rooms for 2 to 8 players (best with 3 or more), or practise against computer players.

Play: https://libyan11.github.io/fake-answers/

Built from `fake-answers.html` with `build-fake-answers.sh`.
